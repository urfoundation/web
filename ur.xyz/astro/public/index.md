# UR (SN25): Decentralized Privacy Network on Bittensor

Source: https://ur.xyz/

UR (SN25) is an open-source privacy network built on Bittensor. The subnet pays the people who contribute to it. Operators run servers and deposit alpha for the traffic they serve. Miners carry encrypted traffic and earn emissions. Validators re-measure the network, verify accuracy, and earn emissions.

## Three roles. One measured network.

### Operators

Operators run the privacy servers and the verification endpoint. They deposit for the traffic they have served, co-sign each measured path, and commit the payout list that splits rewards among their miners. Deposits go into a reserve, and operators never hold anyone else's funds. [Explore Operators](https://ur.xyz/operators) ([markdown](https://ur.xyz/operators.md))

### Miners

Miners carry the traffic. They run nodes that route encrypted traffic for one or more operators, and they are paid from subnet emissions for the traffic they carry and, in the top fleets, for the routable IPs they keep available. [Explore Miners](https://ur.xyz/miners) ([markdown](https://ur.xyz/miners.md))

### Validators

Validators re-measure the network. They run the routing-verification protocol and score each operator pool on demand and measured quality. They earn subnet emissions for accurate scoring. [Explore Validators](https://ur.xyz/validators) ([markdown](https://ur.xyz/validators.md))
