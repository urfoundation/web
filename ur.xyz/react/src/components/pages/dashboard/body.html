Network

Data carried

Data moved through the network for its users, as counted by the operator's monitoring. The big number is the last 24 hours. The period's total and daily average are shown below it. Averages in Gb/s spread it evenly over the period.

Daily users

Accounts that moved data in a 24-hour window. A hosted proxy and the clients behind it count as one account. The big number is the last 24 hours, read when the page opens. The period's average is shown below it and as a dashed line on the chart.

Users per epoch

Accounts that moved data in an epoch, SN25's 7-day payout period, each counted once however many days it was active.

Networks and sign-ups

A network is an account. One account can connect several devices. Sign-ups are networks created in the period.

When traffic flows

The period's average day, hour by hour, in local time. The hourly shape comes from throughput and is scaled so the 24 hours add up to an average day of data carried.

Hourly data carried

In the 1D and 3D views, the last 24 hours and the 48 before them are measured totals. Each hour gets its share of its total in proportion to that hour's throughput.

Running total

Data carried added up day by day from the first day our stats keep.

Providers

Providers online

Devices sharing their connection. The big number is the count online now. The period's average and highest point are shown below it, and the average as a dashed line on the chart.

Countries and cities

Where providers are, never where users are. Locations are shown by country, and no provider is shown individually.

Change

The count now against the count at the start of the period. Where our stats don't reach back that far, no change is shown.

SN25

Payout epochs

SN25 settles payouts every 7 days (50,400 blocks). Users and data carried per epoch use the same boundaries.

Earned by providers

Alpha earned by providers in finished epochs and the current one, still in progress, from the subnet's miner emissions. It shows as pending until the payout is confirmed paid; providers claim it themselves. The payout schedule is on ur.xyz/mainnet. Epochs 0 and 1 were testing, when providers were still paid in USDC.

Operator deposits

Alpha that network operators pay into SN25 for the traffic they use.

Network Capacity Reserve

A wallet held by a 2-of-3 multisig that receives part of SN25's provider emissions through two hotkeys it owns, and funds network capacity.

More stats

Throughput

Data moving through the network each second, counted on every hop of a route, so it runs higher than data carried.

Connected devices

Distinct devices with a live connection, reconnects included.

New connections

New client sessions, reconnects included.

Extenders

Relays that help people connect where direct routes are blocked.

Periods

1D and 3D end at the last full hour, in hourly and 6-hour steps. 7D, 30D and 90D use full UTC days and end at the last full day. Each change compares with the period of the same length just before it. Figures marked Now are live and don't follow the period. Times are in local time.

Sources

  • grafana.bringyour.comNetwork and provider figures, from BringYour's monitoring, with 90 days of history
  • Bittensor mainnetPayout epochs, the reserve and its hotkeys, read through Bittensor's public nodes

Figures are read from these sources each time the page opens (last read ). A copy of each day's totals is kept with the site, so the charts can reach further back than Grafana's 90 days.
